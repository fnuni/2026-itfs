# Release 13.0.0 — 28 October 2026

Reproducibility artifact for **Common-Membership Regret Certificates for Fleet Routing from Finite Endpoints**.
Public release: <https://github.com/fnuni/2026-itfs/releases/tag/v13.0.0>. No DOI is assigned.

The complete artifact contains finite-endpoint models, sharp membership constructions, robust ordered-weighted routing,
certified interpolation, common-membership regret, exact integer strict-mask circulation, 288 complete-domain settings
at 7/8/9 tasks, supporting benchmark and questionnaire studies, protocols, generated tables and independent internal validators.
The mathematical audit checks 384 rational masks and 72 full membership constructions. Certificate replays cover
1,152 method certificates and 1,809 candidate witnesses. Every payload file is listed in PACKAGE_MANIFEST.json.

PL retains the lowest mean regret on the smooth and neutral constructed families. The RB rule improves certified
worst-case regret within explicit robust and nominal budgets; it does not guarantee better mean performance.
The general random-set upper-expectation principle, generic regret and flow integrality are credited to prior work.

Data and completion scenarios are constructed; questionnaire experience is self-reported. Original completed forms
are excluded. No field validation or probabilistic calibration is claimed. Complete heterogeneous routing optima reach
nine tasks; larger scaled-copy studies have a separate scope. The optimistic-feasibility sweep has 52 uncertified
secondary solves, identified separately from certified comparisons. See README.md for reproduction and limitations.
